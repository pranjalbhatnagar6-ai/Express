#Model,veiws,controller + Router
Model - (Data ka sara iske andar hoga)
    Represent your data in your code
    Work with your data (e.g. save, fetch)

veiws -
    Whats the users sees
    Decoupled from your application code

Router-controller :
    Connecting your model and your views
    Contains the "In-between" logic

User -->controller ---__Manupulates__---> Model ---> _Updates_ --> view-->User
